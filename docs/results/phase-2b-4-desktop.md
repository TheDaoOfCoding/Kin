## The two real gaps the design found (S4a: tests first)

`tests/unlink_2b4.rs` was written **before** `remove_device` was touched (it was made `async` first, with the old body, so the tests could call it). What failed on the old code:

| Test | Old code |
|---|---|
| (a) an upload in flight when the phone is removed ends and writes no more | **FAILED**: the `.part` file stayed and the upload went on to completion (nothing in `remove_device` touched it) |
| queued sends of the removed phone | **FAILED**: the item stayed `queued` |
| (b) an already-open TLS connection gets `401` on its next request | passed (authorization is per request: this test is the proof) |
| (c) the WebSocket is closed (`unpair`, then 1000) | passed |
| (d) a fresh connection with the old key is `401` | passed |
| (e) the removal is in the file for a second connection and after a restart | passed (it cannot show the power cut) |

After the fix all six pass. `remove_device` now: (1) **durable removal**: `Db::durable` sets `PRAGMA synchronous = FULL` for the one transaction of `upsert_device` / `remove_device` and puts it back to `NORMAL` (also on an error); tests assert `FULL` inside, `NORMAL` before and after, a rolled-back failed write, and that a second connection to the same file sees the removal at once. A real power cut can't be tested (R38). (2) pending cancels, mirror toasts and icons, presence/ring state and `featureOverrides` go. (3) the connection is told (`unpair` or 4003). (4) **`abort_device`**: running transfers stop (`drop_transfer` with `peer_removed`: a writer stops, a stream ends, `.part` files go by the existing rule). (5) queued items fail as `peer_changed`. (6) the pause is cleared when the last phone goes. A phone that unlinks itself (`unpair`) takes the same path without sending an `unpair` back.

## Differences from the design (also in changelog 58, for the Android coder and the reviewer)

* **`ring.stop` is always acked**, also with the ring switch off (the design's 3.12 said it too could answer `disabled`; 3.8 said "always answered with `ack`"; stopping is never refused). An `error` `disabled` or `rate_limited` to a `ring.start` is not remembered for de-duplication.
* **A nudge suppressed by the receiver's own pause does not disarm** (it fires on the first snapshot after the pause, if the phone is still at 15 % or less and not charging). Pinned by `lowBattery[]`. A fullscreen program, a presentation or quiet time hold the nudge back the same way.
* `remainingMs` of 0 is `bad_request` (the sender sends no `pause` once it ended). `ring.status` needs `presence` on both sides (the design's 3.12), not `ring`.
* **A switch change goes at once** (the share switch, the dev battery): not subject to the 30 s rule. The vectors don't pin it.
* **Resolver and peers.** Every reader goes through `Core::feature_enabled`, and a test fails if code reads a flat key directly. The links decision passes the connection's peer; the automatic clipboard, the media and the notification switches have no connection in hand when they are read and pass none (the default). A later screen of overrides will need those three to pass a peer.
* The phone's pause on an **offline** phone is not shown (forgotten with the connection, as designed); the offline line shows the last battery.
* `presence_view()` reads the database a few times per refresh (the headline phone, `is_paired`): it runs on a state change and every 0.7 s while the drop window is open, which is cheap, but it is not free.

## Known limits and risks

* **R38** can't be tested without a power cut: `synchronous = FULL` for the device list is the fix, the test proves the setting and the visibility, not the disk.
* The battery is **polled every 60 s** (and only while it matters), not event driven: a laptop's level in the phone's notification can be up to a minute old plus the 30 s rule.
* **The windows, the tray menu and the toasts were not looked at** (nothing visible may be shown while a game or launcher runs): the tooltip and status line are unit tested as text; the menu's shape is tested (`Shape`), not its look; the settings and drop windows are TypeScript that compiles.
* `featureOverrides` has no UI and three switches (clipboard, media, notifications) pass no peer yet.
* `ring.start` waits up to 8 s (5 s for the `ack` plus a margin); a ring is never sent again after a missing `ack`, by design.
* A phone that sends a `presence` faster than the bucket (10, one per 3 s) gets one `rate_limited` per 10 s, which an Android that reads it as an error should not retry.
* `presence_peer_hello` creates a connection's state at the first `hello` only; a later `hello` that adds the `presence` capability (an update while connected) starts the snapshot then.
* The Windows half of `images::write_clipboard_image` (the three marks) and of `GetSystemPowerStatus` run for real only in `tools\check-2b4-desktop.ps1` (pending) and in one read-only unit test; this PC has no battery, so the laptop path is a fake.

## Manual checklist for the user (for `docs/TESTING.md`; nothing here was done by an agent)

1. **Look at the tray** with a phone connected: the tooltip ("Kin: Pixel 8 connected · 62% charging"), the status line, and the menu: **Pause Kin ▸** (1 hour / Until tomorrow / Until I resume), **Ring my phone**, **Unlink phone…**. Pause: the dot turns **grey**, the menu says **Resume Kin**, the tooltip says "paused until 14:30". Wait for the end of a 1-hour pause (or use `--dev-clock-offset`): the dot is green again by itself.
2. **A laptop on battery, charging, full, asleep**: the phone's notification (2B.4 Android) shows the right level; unplug and plug while connected (it can take up to a minute).
3. **Low battery**: let the phone fall to 15 % off the charger: one notice "Pixel 8 is at 14% and not charging", not a second one at 13 %, one more after charging above 20 % and falling again. Open a fullscreen game: no notice over it.
4. **Ring** a real phone (silent, vibrate, Priority-only, total silence, with Bluetooth headphones): the Stop button on the PC, on the phone, and the 30 s end; "Ringing, but Do Not Disturb is on total silence".
5. **Pause on the phone**: its Pause tile: the PC says "phone paused", the PC sends no automatic clipboard. **Pause on the PC**: a text, a link and a photo sent by hand from the phone arrive in the history with **no toast, no clipboard change, no browser**; "3 received while paused" in the left-click window; resume: everything flows.
6. **Unlink**: Settings > Phone > Unlink: the confirmation text; the phone shows its "pair again" state; **start a big upload from the phone and unlink during it** (the phone's progress stops, no file is left); **unlink while the phone is off**, switch it on later: it can't connect; a power cut right after an unlink: the phone must still be gone (R38).
7. **Screenshots** (needs the Android part): take a screenshot: a notice "Screenshot from Pixel 8", the picture on the clipboard (Ctrl+V pastes it), **and Win+V does not list it**; a camera photo is saved with "Photo from Pixel 8" and is **not** on the clipboard; while paused: saved quietly, nothing shown.
8. `Kin.exe --pause 1h`, `--pause resume`, `--ring`, `--ring-stop` from a terminal.
