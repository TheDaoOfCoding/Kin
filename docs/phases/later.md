# Later

These get revisited after Phase 2. They aren't promises.

- **Screen mirroring and remote control.** Android needs MediaProjection for the video and an AccessibilityService for the input. It's heavy, drains battery, and the Play Store scrutinizes accessibility use. Projects like scrcpy do it over ADB, which is worth studying first.
- **Phone as webcam.** Check how much overlaps with what Windows 11 already offers for some phones.
- **SMS and calls from the PC.**
- **Keyboard and mouse sharing** between PC and tablet.
- **iPhone and iPad.** This needs a device and a Mac to build on.
- **Optional account and paid tier:** cloud inbox, higher bridge limits, and sync across networks. The free core stays account-free.
